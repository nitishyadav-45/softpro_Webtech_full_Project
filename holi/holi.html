🌈 The Festival of Colors 🌈

Holi celebrates the triumph of good over evil and the arrival of spring

Holi 2026

March 10, 2026

🏺

Holika Dahan

The night before Holi, bonfires are lit to signify the burning of the demoness Holika and the triumph of good over evil.

🎨

Rangwali Holi

The main day of celebration where people play with colors, water guns, and colored water, spreading joy everywhere.

🍬

Gujiya & Sweets

Traditional sweets like Gujiya, Malpua, and Thandai are prepared and shared with family and friends.
