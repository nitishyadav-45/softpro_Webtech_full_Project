Government Ideas for "Vocal for Local"

Innovative strategies the government could implement to empower local businesses, artisans, and startups in 2025 and beyond.

Local Brand Certification

“Proudly Local” or “Made in My District” certification builds trust and boosts small producers’ sales.

Digital Marketplace

An official app for artisans, farmers, and startups to sell products safely with government-backed delivery.

Localpreneurship in Schools

Students adopt local businesses to help with digital marketing, design, and mini start-up challenges.

Geo-Tagging Local Businesses

Map registered local businesses digitally so people can explore industries in their area via an app.

Local Tourism + Products

Encourage tourists to buy district souvenirs & set up ODOP kiosks at airports and tourist sites.

Festival Campaigns

Promote local gifts & artisan products during Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Independence Day.

Vocal For Local 🇮🇳

Supporting local artisans, farmers, and small industries to build a self-reliant India.
